---

Create ONE character reference sheet image (wide 16:9, 3 columns x 2 rows, six equal panels, thin light-grey panel borders, a small plain label under each panel). It shows ONE frozen pose of ONE 3D character, the IDLE combat stance of the hero "Ninja" from a friendly mobile tower-defense game. Toy-like stylized character and prop.

THE MOST IMPORTANT RULE: treat the character as a real 3D figurine frozen on a turntable. All six panels show the SAME figurine with the SAME pose. Only the camera moves. Every joint angle, the stance width, which foot is forward, the arm positions, the kunai angle and the scarf shape must match exactly between panels. Do not re-pose, do not make the pose symmetric, do not improve it per view.

CHARACTER DESIGN (match the attached model renders exactly): chibi proportions, total height about 4 heads (head = 1/4 of the height), huge round head with no face details, smooth white low-poly body with flat shading, short arms (upper arm + forearm shorter than the head is tall x 1.2), mitten-like hands, a red scarf wrapped around the neck with TWO long red tails.
KUNAI (match the attached kunai render): dark steel diamond-shaped blade, red wrapped grip, small dark ring at the pommel. It is very big for this character: the blade alone is longer than the whole arm.

THE POSE (bottom of a calm breath, ready ninja guard; "left" and "right" are the character's own sides):
- Stance: crouched, knees clearly bent and pointing over the toes, feet clearly wider than the hips (about 2 hip-widths). LEFT foot a little forward, toes pointing at the enemy; RIGHT foot behind, toes turned out about 30 degrees. Both feet flat on the ground.
- Hips lowered, pelvis turned slightly so the LEFT shoulder leads toward the enemy.
- Torso leaning forward about 12 degrees from the hips, back evenly curved, not hunched; chest turned back a little toward the viewer.
- Head looking straight ahead at the enemy, chin slightly down.
- LEFT hand holds the kunai in REVERSE GRIP (icepick): the fist is in front of the upper chest, just under the scarf, about a forearm's length in front of the body; the left elbow is bent and points out to the side and slightly down. The blade comes out of the pinky side of the fist and runs back and down along the outside of the left forearm at about 30 degrees below horizontal; the tip ends beside the left hip, outside the body, never touching the legs or the torso.
- RIGHT hand is EMPTY, loosely curled as if holding an invisible second kunai in reverse grip; the right arm is pushed back, elbow slightly bent, the fist beside and a little behind the right hip, clear of the body.
- Scarf: very light breeze. The two tails hang behind the back in a soft S-curve, drooping gently downward, slightly apart, never touching the ground or the legs.

THE SIX PANELS (keep the figurine at the same size and the feet on the same horizontal ground line in panels 1-4):
1. "FRONT" - strict orthographic front elevation, camera at mid-body height, looking horizontally at the direction the hips face, no perspective. The kunai hand is on the RIGHT of the panel.
2. "LEFT SIDE" - strict orthographic profile from the character's left (kunai side); the character faces the LEFT edge of the panel. The right arm and right leg are mostly hidden behind the body.
3. "BACK" - strict orthographic back elevation; the scarf tails are seen from behind.
4. "RIGHT SIDE" - strict orthographic profile from the character's right (empty-hand side); the character faces the RIGHT edge of the panel.
5. "3/4 FRONT" - perspective view from the front-left at eye level, like the first attached concept image.
6. "GAME CAMERA" - mobile tower-defense camera: looking DOWN at 45 degrees from above and behind-right of the character, the character facing toward the top of the panel; the kunai line and both arms clearly separated from the body silhouette.

STYLE: same white low-poly flat-shaded 3D look as the references, plain flat medium-grey background in every panel, flat even lighting, a very soft contact shadow under the feet only, no text except the six short labels, no extra characters, no extra weapons, no effects.
